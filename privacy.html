Last updated: May 2026

FOCS Properties LLC ("FOCS Properties," "we," "us," or "our") respects the privacy of visitors to our website and users of our contact forms. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information you provide through this website.

We collect information you voluntarily submit through our investor inquiry form, including:

Information about investment interest and investor type is collected solely to determine whether we can share private offering materials with you in compliance with applicable securities laws. This information is not used for any other purpose.

Retention

We retain contact and inquiry information for a reasonable period to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected and to comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to request that we update, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To make such a request, email us at info@focsproperties.com.
